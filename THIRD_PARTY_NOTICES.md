## Empire

Reference: <https://github.com/thebuggeddev/empire>

Empire was used only as a product-design and architecture reference for data-driven collections, CSS scenery behind a transparent WebGL layer, spatial hotspots and camera focus transitions. No Empire source code, art or assets were copied. At the time of inspection, its public repository did not include an explicit license, so this implementation deliberately limits reuse to general concepts.
